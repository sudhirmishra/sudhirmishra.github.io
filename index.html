Architecture of an Extensible Production Email-Security Platform

Intake, multi-engine threat detection, automated remediation, and extensible management planes
Live Mail Flow Loop Raw SVG
STAGE 1

Intake & Pre-Processing

Messages enter from the Internet via the Security Platform Gateway into a resilient Parser & Queue, dissecting messages into RFC 822 headers, HTML/plain-text bodies, and binary attachments.

STAGE 2

Multi-Engine Threat Analysis

Parallel evaluation through pluggable engines: URL emulation & threat feeds, attachment detonation (Talos/Bitdefender sandboxing), and header/content heuristics (Vade Secure, DMARC/SPF), scored by the Threat Decision Engine.

STAGE 3

Remediation & Action

Identified threats trigger automated quarantine, blocking, or post-delivery inbox retraction. Verified safe emails are routed to corporate inboxes while all processing telemetry streams to the SQL/BerkeleyDB audit store.

STAGE 4

Administration & Extensibility

Dual administrative interfaces (Web-Based GUI Dashboard and CLI) enable policy orchestration, quarantine inspection, and hot-swappable adapter registration for new scanning engines.
